## Overview

This project involved configuring AdGuard Home on my OPNsense firewall to
provide DNS filtering, while maintaining local hostname resolution through
Unbound.

## Goal

I wanted to reduce my reliance on a browser ad-blocking extension and understand
how DNS filtering works within my home network.

## What I worked on

- Installing AdGuard Home on OPNsense.
- Configuring DNS filtering with the default AdGuard filter.
- Configuring local hostname resolution using Unbound and the home.arpa domain.
- Investigating DNS behaviour and adjusting the configuration.
- Testing blocked domains and local hostname lookups.

## Verification

I checked that a blocked advertising domain, doubleclick.net, resolved to
0.0.0.0, and separately confirmed that local hostnames resolved correctly.

## What I learned

The project helped me understand the relationship between DNS filtering and
local name resolution, and the importance of testing each part of a
configuration when troubleshooting.
